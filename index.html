Inside the making of an AI music video

A song. A whole world.
A very curious process.

Human direction, AI imagination, and a set of homemade creative tools to bring it all together.

Make the song→Map the music→Build the characters→Direct the shots→Refine the look
01 / Turn a song into a story

Every frame has a job.

Heartbreak, old friends, rediscovered curiosity, and a world that answers back. We shaped the story around the song, then built a player to watch it unfold.

Each of the 138 shots has direction for action, angle, camera movement, emotion, body language, continuity and its place in the music.

02 / Find the pulse

The music draws the map.

The song was made with Suno. Our MP3 tools charted its pulse and energy, helping us place discoveries, scene changes and the final crescendo.

575 detected beats. An estimated pulse of 127.51 BPM. Click-track playback lets us check the cuts against the recording.

Python + librosaOnset analysisTempo ≠ energy
03 / Catch every word

The song had a surprise.

We separated vocals from the music, cross-checked speech recognition, then aligned the lyrics to the recording. The result: 45 lyric lines and 223 word spans, with uncertain passages flagged for playback review.

It caught an extra final “And every colour comes alive” repeat missing from the supplied lyrics. That changed the ending.

04 / Give the world a look

One film. Many moods.

Midnight indigo and warm amber connect the film. Scene palettes guide the light: cosy apartment lamps, blue electronics, selective arcade neon, and magic that gets to pop.

Stable skin, hair and wardrobe colours. Lighting judged scene by scene.

05 / Make the detail sing

More detail. Same movie.

ImageGen redraws used the existing shots as references. We compared composition, expression, props, skin tone and exposure before choosing the new artwork.

The review tool has side-by-side views, a draggable wipe and press-and-hold originals. Its floating CRT panel adds browser-only scanlines, bloom and colour controls.
